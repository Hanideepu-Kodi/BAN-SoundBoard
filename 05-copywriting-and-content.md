# 05: Copywriting and Content

This document outlines the copywriting guidelines and specific UI text for the soundboard application, ensuring a consistent and engaging tone of voice.

---

## 1. Tone of Voice

Our voice is **Creative, Playful, and Clear**.

*   **Creative:** We use imaginative and evocative language to inspire our users. We avoid corporate jargon and bland phrasing.
*   **Playful:** We embrace the fun and entertaining nature of soundboards. Our copy can be witty and humorous, but never silly or unprofessional.
*   **Clear:** Above all, our communication is simple, direct, and easy to understand. We guide the user and make sure they always know what to do next.

---

## 2. Messaging Strategy

*   **Acquisition (For new visitors):** The message is about limitless creative potential and the joy of discovery. We highlight the ease of use and the vibrant community.
*   **Onboarding (For new users):** The message is welcoming and encouraging. We guide them through the first key actions, like creating a playlist or uploading a sound, to build their confidence.
*   **Engagement (For returning users):** The message is about continuous discovery and sharing. We encourage them to explore new sounds, build their collection, and share their creations with the world.

---

## 3. UI Copy

### Homepage
*   **Headline:** The Universe of Sound, at Your Fingertips.
*   **Subheading:** Discover, create, and share the perfect sound for any moment.
*   **Primary CTA:** Start Creating for Free
*   **Trending Sounds Headline:** Hear What's Hot

### Registration / Login
*   **Sign Up Title:** Join the Soundwave
*   **Sign Up CTA:** Sign Up with Google
*   **Log In Title:** Welcome Back
*   **Log In CTA:** Log In with Google

### Dashboard
*   **Welcome Message:** Hi [Username], what will you create today?
*   **My Playlists (Empty State):** Your stage is empty. Create your first playlist to start the show!
*   **My Sounds (Empty State):** Silence... for now. Upload your first sound and make some noise!
*   **Create New Playlist Button:** New Playlist
*   **Upload Sound Button:** Upload Sound

### Upload Page
*   **Page Title:** Bring Your Sounds to Life
*   **Drag-and-Drop Area:** Drag & drop your audio file here, or **browse files**.
*   **File Constraints:** Max 1 minute. MP3, AAC, WAV, or OGG.
*   **Title Field Label:** Sound Title
*   **Description Field Label:** Description (optional)
*   **Tags Field Label:** Tags (comma-separated)
*   **Visibility Label:** Sharing
*   **Public Option:** Public (Visible to everyone)
*   **Private Option:** Private (Only you can see this)
*   **Submit Button:** Save Sound

### Notifications & Feedback
*   **Playlist Created:** Playlist "[Playlist Name]" created! Time to add some sounds.
*   **Sound Added to Playlist:** Success! Sound added to "[Playlist Name]".
*   **Sound Uploaded:** Your sound is live! Thanks for contributing to the community.
*   **Error (Generic):** Oops! Something went wrong. Please try that again.
*   **Copied to Clipboard:** Copied to clipboard. Now go share it!
