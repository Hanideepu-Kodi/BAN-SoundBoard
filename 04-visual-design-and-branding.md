# 04: Visual Design and Branding

This document outlines the visual identity for the soundboard application, including the color palette, typography, iconography, and high-fidelity mockups of the key screens.

---

## 1. Branding

*   **Brand Name:** Soundwave (tentative)
*   **Logo:** A stylized soundwave, clean and modern. The logo will be versatile and work well in both large and small formats.
*   **Brand Personality:** Creative, playful, sophisticated, and user-friendly.

---

## 2. Color Palette

*   **Primary Color:** A deep, vibrant blue (#0052FF) will be used for primary calls to action, links, and accents. This color is energetic and trustworthy.
*   **Secondary Color:** A bright, cheerful orange (#FFA500) will be used for secondary actions and to highlight important information.
*   **Neutral Colors:** A range of grays will be used for text, backgrounds, and UI elements.
    *   `#121212` (Almost Black) for primary text.
    *   `#F5F5F5` (Light Gray) for backgrounds.
    *   `#E0E0E0` (Medium Gray) for borders and dividers.
*   **Accent Colors:** A soft green (#00C49A) for success messages and a vibrant red (#FF4136) for error messages.

---

## 3. Typography

*   **Primary Typeface:** "Inter" will be used for all headings and body text. It is a clean, modern, and highly legible sans-serif typeface that works well on screens.
*   **Headings:**
    *   H1: 48px, Bold
    *   H2: 36px, Bold
    *   H3: 24px, Bold
*   **Body Text:** 16px, Regular
*   **UI Elements:** 14px, Medium

---

## 4. Iconography

*   A custom set of icons will be designed to ensure a consistent and unique visual style. The icons will be simple, clean, and easily recognizable.
*   Icons will be used for actions like "play," "pause," "add to playlist," "upload," "settings," etc.

---

## 5. High-Fidelity Mockups (Descriptions)

*   **Homepage:**
    *   A full-width hero image with a subtle soundwave animation.
    *   The primary blue color will be used for the "Create Your Free Account" button, making it stand out.
    *   Curated playlists and trending sounds will be displayed in clean, modern cards with rounded corners and a slight drop shadow.
*   **Explore Page:**
    *   The search bar will be prominent and easy to use.
    *   Sound cards will have a hover effect that reveals a play button and an "add to playlist" icon.
    *   Filters will be presented as dropdowns or buttons, depending on the number of options.
*   **Playlist Page:**
    *   The playlist header will be visually engaging, with the playlist title in a large, bold font.
    *   The sound list will be clean and easy to scan, with clear visual cues for playing and adding sounds.
*   **User Dashboard:**
    *   The sidebar navigation will use icons and text for clarity.
    *   The main content area will be spacious and uncluttered, with a clear focus on the user's content.
*   **Upload Sound Page:**
    *   The drag-and-drop area will be large and inviting, with clear instructions.
    *   The form fields will be well-spaced and easy to read.
    *   The "Upload" button will be the primary blue color and will be disabled until all required fields are filled.
